GLG Home: 25 prototypes

Girls Lift Girls is a women's fitness community, "Strength in Community", with chapters in Hong Kong, Dubai, Sydney, Brisbane, Gold Coast, Heidelberg and Shanghai.

This is the home screen a member sees when she opens the app on her phone. In a few seconds she should know what's coming up in her city, how to book a spot, and the latest news from her chapter.

Every version uses the same fictional content: Jasmine, a Hong Kong member, with five upcoming events, two chapter announcements and one global update. The early versions try very different directions. The later ones combine what worked and narrow down to a final pick at v25.

The versions

v01–v05 go wide with themed, event-first screens. v06 onwards moves to movement-led home pages that add something personal, your booked events and a mix of community updates, because the early ones read like an events list rather than a home.

v01 · Departures

Events as a split-flap transit board in time order; tap a row to book. Chapter news runs as a ticker.
v02 · Order Sheet

A yum cha tick-sheet: tick several events and book them all with one running-total button.
v03 · Tape Shelf

80s mixtape: the next event is a cassette whose reels spin when you book; the rest sit on a swipeable shelf.
v04 · Zine Feed

Punk zine energy kept to the ransom headline and stickers; events sit in chunky, tidy offset-shadow cards.
v06 · Energy Check-in

First real home page: "How's your energy?" picks your events, with stats, your bookings and a community digest.
v07 · Doing Things

Verbs lead everything: Lift. Hike. Run. Pastel tiles for events, verb-led stats and community cards.
