GLG Home: 25 prototypes

Girls Lift Girls is a women's fitness community, "Strength in Community", with chapters in Hong Kong, Dubai, Sydney, Brisbane, Gold Coast, Heidelberg and Shanghai.

This is the home screen a member sees when she opens the app on her phone. In a few seconds she should know what's coming up in her city, how to book a spot, and the latest news from her chapter.

Every version uses the same fictional content: Jasmine, a Hong Kong member, with five upcoming events, two chapter announcements and one global update. The early versions try very different directions. The later ones combine what worked and narrow down to a final pick at v25.

The versions

v01–v05 go wide with themed, event-first screens. v06–v10 become real home pages, with something personal, your booked events and a mix of community updates, each built around a different feature (energy check-in, verbs, stories, a map, buddies). They started to look alike, so v06, v08 and v10 were restyled for distinct moods (calm, hype, warm), and v11–v15 push tone further: strong, girly, together (sporty, photo-led), cool and chic. v16–v19 explore on-brand directions for the real Girls Lift Girls brand (purple and white, Oswald-style capitals, group photos, "stronger together") each a full home page with Morning, Jasmine, your streak, what you're signed up for, what to join next and the community feed. From v20 the versions converge on a final pick.

v01 · Departures

Events as a split-flap transit board in time order; tap a row to book. Chapter news runs as a ticker.
v02 · Order Sheet

A yum cha tick-sheet: tick several events and book them all with one running-total button.
v03 · Tape Shelf

80s mixtape: the next event is a cassette whose reels spin when you book; the rest sit on a swipeable shelf.
v04 · Zine Feed

Punk zine energy kept to the ransom headline and stickers; events sit in chunky, tidy offset-shadow cards.
v05 · Morning Briefing

A personal note from the chapter lead with an inline booking link, then a calm at-a-glance list. The first version to speak to Jasmine directly.
v06 · Energy Check-in

Calm and mindful: Optima, sage and sand, and breathing circles for "How does your body feel?", which picks your events. Gentle tone throughout.
v07 · Doing Things

Fully pastel and playful: verbs lead everything (Lift. Hike. Run.), a rainbow headline and pastel tiles for stats, events and community. No black anywhere.
v08 · Stories

Loud and sporty: black and volt, condensed Impact caps, "Let's go, Jasmine." Story rings open a working viewer; events are swipeable full-bleed cards.
v09 · City Map

Hong Kong as the home screen: event pins on a map, with a bottom sheet for your plans, nearby sessions and place-tagged community news.
v10 · Never Alone

Warm and sisterly: italic Hoefler serif, handwritten notes on lined paper, a taped-on buddy match ("Chloe is going solo too"), your crew, and cheering your girls on.
v11 · Strong

Raw and hard-edged: Arial Black caps on concrete, a coach's voice ("Jasmine. Saturday. 2 spots."), a PR board, and Lift a Million as a loaded barbell.
v12 · Girly

Pink gingham, script headings and bows: a beating-heart streak, invitation cards for your plans, polaroid events to swipe, and "Girl talk" chat bubbles.
v13 · Together

Sporty and together, in purple: your week as a bar chart, route maps for your bookings, photo cards for group sessions to join, a club challenge leaderboard and club news.
v14 · Cool

Streetwear drop culture: lowercase mono voice, a holographic member card, events as numbered "drops" with live countdowns, and a minimal feed.
v15 · Chic

Quiet luxury: Didot italics on ivory, hairline rules, an itinerary with dotted leaders, sessions "to reserve", and the global update as a save-the-date card.
v16 · Strength in Community

Brand-faithful: GLG purple and white, Oswald-style capitals, a full-bleed group photo saying "Morning, Jasmine.", streak card, photo tickets, photo events to join, a community feed and the GLG values band.
v19 · Lavender Drop

v14 meets v7: lowercase "morning, jasmine.", a pastel holographic member card with your streak, claimed sessions with countdowns, photo drops, and an Instagram-style community mosaic.
v20 · Night Session

v8 meets v13 after dark: photo story rings that open full-screen, "MORNING, JASMINE." in big caps, a streak ring, full-bleed photo cards for what you're locked into, a swipeable photo deck to join, and the squad feed.
