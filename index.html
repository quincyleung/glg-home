MPCS 51238 · Assignment 1

GLG Home: 25 prototypes

Girls Lift Girls is a women's fitness community with chapters in Hong Kong, Dubai, Sydney, Brisbane, Gold Coast, Heidelberg and Shanghai.

This is the home screen a member sees when she opens the app on her phone. In a few seconds she should know what she has booked, what is coming up in her city, and the latest news from her chapter.

Every version uses the same fictional content: Jasmine, a Hong Kong member, with five upcoming events, two chapter announcements and one global update. The early versions try very different directions. The later ones combine what worked and narrow down to a final pick at v25.

The versions

v01–v05 go wide with themed, event-first screens. v06–v15 turn them into real home pages and explore mood and tone. v16–v20 learn from the real Girls Lift Girls brand. v21–v24 converge on the favourites, and v25 is the final pick.

v01 · Departures

Events as a split-flap transit board in time order; tap a row to book. Chapter news runs as a ticker.
v02 · Order Sheet

A yum cha tick-sheet: tick several events and book them all with one running-total button.
v03 · Tape Shelf

80s mixtape: the next event is a cassette whose reels spin when you book; the rest sit on a swipeable shelf.
v04 · Zine Feed

Punk zine energy kept to the ransom headline and stickers; events sit in chunky, tidy offset-shadow cards.
v05 · Morning Briefing

A personal note from the chapter lead with an inline booking link, then a calm at-a-glance list. The first version to speak to Jasmine directly.
v06 · Energy Check-in

Calm and mindful: Optima, sage and sand, and breathing circles for "How does your body feel?", which picks your events. Gentle tone throughout.
v07 · Doing Things

Fully pastel and playful: verbs lead everything (Lift. Hike. Run.), a rainbow headline and pastel tiles for stats, events and community. No black anywhere.
v08 · Stories

Loud and sporty: black and volt, condensed Impact caps, "Let's go, Jasmine." Story rings open a working viewer; events are swipeable full-bleed cards.
v09 · City Map

Hong Kong as the home screen: event pins on a map, with a bottom sheet for your plans, nearby sessions and place-tagged community news.
v10 · Never Alone

Warm and sisterly: italic Hoefler serif, handwritten notes on lined paper, a taped-on buddy match ("Chloe is going solo too"), your crew, and cheering your girls on.
v11 · Strong

Raw and hard-edged: Arial Black caps on concrete, a coach's voice ("Jasmine. Saturday. 2 spots."), a PR board, and Lift a Million as a loaded barbell.
v12 · Girly

Pink gingham, script headings and bows: a beating-heart streak, invitation cards for your plans, polaroid events to swipe, and "Girl talk" chat bubbles.
v13 · Together

Sporty and together, in purple: your week as a bar chart, route maps for your bookings, photo cards for group sessions to join, a club challenge leaderboard and club news.
v14 · Cool

Streetwear drop culture: lowercase mono voice, a holographic member card, events as numbered "drops" with live countdowns, and a minimal feed.
v15 · Chic

Quiet luxury: Didot italics on ivory, hairline rules, an itinerary with dotted leaders, sessions "to reserve", and the global update as a save-the-date card.
v16 · Strength in Community

Brand-faithful: GLG purple and white, Oswald-style capitals, a full-bleed group photo saying "Morning, Jasmine.", streak card, photo tickets, photo events to join, a community feed and the GLG values band.
v17 · Week Ahead

Same brand, organised by time: a streak heatmap, a sticky date scroller, and one timeline where your bookings, sessions to join and community moments sit on the day they happen.
v18 · Cover Story

Same brand as an editorial poster: a photo-collage cover with "MORNING, JASMINE." on purple labels, a giant 3 for your streak, numbered events each tied to a GLG value, and a member pull quote.
v19 · Quiet

Minimal: just type, white space and one purple. Illustrated avatars instead of photos, a dot-per-week streak, open spots as seat dots, and the community list folded away.
v20 · Orbit

Avatar-led: you at the centre with your streak as a ring, your crew orbiting (tap anyone with a pink dot for their news), and every session drawn as seats you can tap to take.
v21 · Lavender Drop

First convergence step: a holographic member card that flips to a check-in QR, a one-line "morning, jasmine.", "you're going", a "psst, your friends are going" nudge, equal "coming up" cards, and a compact "from the community" snippet with hearts, comments and joinable community challenges.
v22 · Stronger Together

v21's features in v16's style, as first combined: a short photo hero, a simple white streak card with Check in → QR, the psst nudge, big stacked photo cards with who's going, the community snippet and an icon tab bar. (Its later refinements became v25.)
v23 · Together, Converged

Every v22 decision in v13's sporty style: Helvetica-heavy sentence case, a sticky GLG top bar, the week as a bar chart, route maps on your bookings, and swipeable rows for what you're going to, sessions to join and community posts.
v24 · Orbit, Converged

Every v22 decision in v20's avatar-led style: friends orbit you, badged with their next session (tap to join her); tap yourself for the streak card. Simple white photo cards with three faces each, on a soft glow.
v25 · Final: Stronger Together

The pick: v22 polished with the best of v21, v23 and v24: live day/hour countdowns, tap any friend's face to join her, route maps in details, one psst nudge, a motivational line to end on, and the real Oswald + Arimo fonts.

Final pick: v25 · Stronger Together

Open v25 → (and v25 with nothing booked, to see the empty state)

v25 is v22 polished. It won because it does the three jobs in the brief within a few seconds, and it looks and sounds like Girls Lift Girls:

  • What's on in her city: "You're going" (with live countdowns in days and hours) and "Join a session" are side-by-side swipeable rows of photo cards. Each has one line of date · time · place, the instructor's face and who's going.
  • How to book: every session has its price and a Join button on the card. The "psst, your friends are going" nudge and tapping any friend's face turn booking into "join her", which is the brand's "never lift alone" idea.
  • Latest from the chapter: "From the community" shows the three newest posts, announcements and challenges, with hearts, comments and Join challenge.
  • On brand: GLG purple on a calm light-purple page, the real Oswald and Arimo type from the GLG website, a group photo of women at the top, and an inclusive voice.

How it got here: v01–v05 went wide with event-first themes; v06–v15 explored mood and tone; v16–v20 explored on-brand directions after studying the GLG website; v21–v24 converged (v21's holographic card and community snippet, v22's brand style, then v22's decisions applied to v13 and v20 to test them); v25 combines v22 with the best of v21, v23 and v24.
